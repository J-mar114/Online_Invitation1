<!-- README: Audio Setup -->

To use wedding music on your invitation:

1. Download or prepare a wedding music file (MP3 format)
2. Save it as "wedding-music.mp3" in the audio/ folder
3. The music will appear as a playable button in the bottom-right corner

Example sources for royalty-free wedding music:
- Unsplash Sounds
- Free Music Archive
- YouTube Audio Library
- Epidemic Sound (free tier)
- Pixabay Music

The HTML already references: audio/wedding-music.mp3

Just replace this path with your own music file!

Note: Modern browsers require user interaction to play audio with sound,
so the play button won't auto-play music on page load (this is intentional).
Users must click the music button to play the audio.

GLASSMORPHISM UI

Make glassmorphism one of the main design elements.

Use:
- backdrop-filter: blur()
- Semi-transparent backgrounds
- Soft borders
- Subtle shadows
- Inner highlights
- Transparent gradients
- Soft glow effects

Glass cards should look like frosted glass.
